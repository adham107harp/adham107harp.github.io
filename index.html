Step 4 · Research

Two pipelines. One verdict.

A Modular Dual-Pipeline Anomaly Detection Architecture for IoT Security: Combining Gradient Boosting, Isolation Forest, and Decision Fusion

ITC-Egypt 2026, Cairo · IEEE Xplore · with Tasneem Saleh, Khaled Elhosiny and Marwa Anany

  1. Device sensors go through a supervised XGBoost pipeline.
  2. Network traffic goes through an unsupervised Isolation Forest.
  3. Decision fusion merges both into a four-tier risk score.

Risk tier: Low

An illustration of the architecture, not the trained model.

My part: auditing the paper against its notebook before submission, running the revisions after peer review, adding the fusion-ablation and sensitivity experiments, and building the conference deck.
